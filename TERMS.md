**4. What Kannu does on your PC**

Kannu watches the AI coding agents you use and shows their status. To do that it reads their session files and status files on your PC, and, only when you ask it to, it adds its hook to an agent's settings files. It keeps the previous version of each settings file it changes as a ".kannu-backup" file beside it, and it removes its hooks when you turn them off or uninstall Kannu. Kannu does not send your agents' conversations anywhere.
